🫀 ASCVD Risk Prediction for RA patients

This tool provides an individualized prediction of ASCVD for RA patients using an offline approximation of the final XGBoost model. The prediction is intended for research demonstration and should not replace clinical judgment.

Patient information

Input

CACS is entered as the original CAC score and converted internally to Log(CACS+1). CRP should be entered in mg/L, and mTSS should range from 0 to 280.

Prediction output

Result
--%
Risk tiers: <23.0%, 23.0–46.8%, ≥46.8%

ROC-derived three-tier risk stratification: low risk <23.0%, intermediate risk 23.0%–46.8%, and high risk ≥46.8%. The lower cutoff corresponds to 90% sensitivity, and the upper cutoff maximizes the Youden index.

Approximate feature contributions

Explainability
Increases predicted risk

Contributions are additive approximations from a distilled surrogate of the final XGBoost model.

This offline webpage is intended for research demonstration and should be externally validated before clinical use.
